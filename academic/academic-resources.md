# Academic Resources

> Use this page when Jasper wants support, research, tutoring, or academic community.

## Quick Answer

- For math help, start with the SQRC.
- For math community, try Math Club.
- For summer research, look at the Rogers Science Research Program.
- For tutoring work, check SAAB tutoring and student employment.
- For bigger academic planning, talk with an advisor and faculty early.

## Lewis & Clark Resources

- [Mathematical Sciences Opportunities](https://college.lclark.edu/departments/mathematical_sciences/opportunities/) — Research, internships, events, and opportunities for math students.
- [L&C Math Club](https://college.lclark.edu/live/profiles/4018-math-club) — Talks, social events, Pi Day, Open Math Night, chess, and Pi Mu Epsilon activities.
- [John S. Rogers Science Research Program](https://college.lclark.edu/science/opportunities/rogers/) — Paid summer research with Lewis & Clark faculty.
- [Symbolic and Quantitative Resource Center](https://college.lclark.edu/departments/mathematical_sciences/sqrc/) — Drop-in support for math, statistics, data, and quantitative courses.
- [Become a SAAB Tutor](https://college.lclark.edu/academics/support/saab-tutoring/becoming-a-tutor/) — Paid peer tutoring after demonstrating proficiency in an L&C course.

## Good First Moves

- [ ] Visit Math Club at least once.
- [ ] Find the SQRC before the first exam panic.
- [ ] Ask a professor what strong first-year math students do outside class.
- [ ] Keep notes on interesting problems, projects, and talks.
- [ ] Look at summer research options before spring gets busy.

## Professional Math

- [UO Mathematics Research Areas](https://naturalsciences.uoregon.edu/mathematics/research#research-interests) — Useful for seeing possible doctoral research areas.
- [UO Mathematics Research Opportunities](https://naturalsciences.uoregon.edu/mathematics/hands-on/research) — Reading courses, directed reading, and undergraduate math lectures.
- [Joint Mathematics Meetings](https://www.ams.org/grants-awards/travel-grants/grad-students/emp-student-JMM) — A major professional math meeting; travel support may be available.

## If Something Goes Wrong

If a class starts sliding:

- [ ] Go to office hours.
- [ ] Use the SQRC.
- [ ] Ask whether study groups exist.
- [ ] Tell the professor what you are trying.
- [ ] Talk with advising before deadlines pass.
