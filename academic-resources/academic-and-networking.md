# Academic and Networking Resources

## Lewis & Clark

- [Mathematical Sciences Opportunities](https://college.lclark.edu/departments/mathematical_sciences/opportunities/)
  - Research, internships, departmental events, and opportunities for mathematics students.

- [L&C Math Club](https://college.lclark.edu/live/profiles/4018-math-club)
  - Academic talks, social events, Pi Day, Open Math Night, chess, and Pi Mu Epsilon activities.

- [John S. Rogers Science Research Program](https://college.lclark.edu/science/opportunities/rogers/)
  - Paid summer research with Lewis & Clark faculty, including Mathematical Sciences projects.

- [Symbolic and Quantitative Resource Center](https://college.lclark.edu/departments/mathematical_sciences/sqrc/)
  - Drop-in support for mathematics, statistics, data, quantitative courses, and study groups.

- [Become a SAAB Tutor](https://college.lclark.edu/academics/support/saab-tutoring/becoming-a-tutor/)
  - Paid peer-tutoring opportunities after demonstrating proficiency in an L&C course.

- [Russian Club and Russian Conversation Table](https://college.lclark.edu/offices/interactive_learning_center/foreign-language-clubs-/russian-club-activities-/)
  - Informal Russian practice, cultural events, meals, concerts, and social activities.

## University of Oregon and Professional Mathematics

- [UO Mathematics Research Areas](https://naturalsciences.uoregon.edu/mathematics/research#research-interests)
  - Use to identify possible doctoral research areas and prospective faculty.

- [UO Mathematics Research Opportunities](https://naturalsciences.uoregon.edu/mathematics/hands-on/research)
  - Includes reading courses, the Directed Reading Program, and undergraduate mathematics lectures.

- [Joint Mathematics Meetings](https://www.ams.org/grants-awards/travel-grants/grad-students/emp-student-JMM)
  - Major professional mathematics meeting; travel assistance may be available.
