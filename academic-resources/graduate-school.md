# Graduate School Resources

- [UO Mathematics Graduate Admissions](https://naturalsciences.uoregon.edu/mathematics/apply/graduate-admissions)
  - Admissions expectations, application components, deadlines, and recommended preparation.

- [UO Mathematics PhD Requirements](https://naturalsciences.uoregon.edu/mathematics/graduate-programs/phd)
  - Coursework, qualifying examinations, advisor selection, language proficiency, and dissertation requirements.

  - Graduate teaching and research appointments, tuition waivers, stipends, insurance, and travel support.

- [UO Mathematics Career and Professional Development](https://naturalsciences.uoregon.edu/mathematics/graduate-programs/career)

## National Resources

- [NSF Graduate Research Fellowship Program](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/grfp-nsf-graduate-research-fellowship-program)
  - Major fellowship for students entering or beginning research-based STEM graduate programs.

- [MathJobs.org](https://www.mathjobs.org/jobs)
  - Academic mathematics positions; primarily useful during graduate school.

## Long-Term Reference

- [NSF Mathematical Sciences Postdoctoral Research Fellowships](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/msprf-mathematical-sciences-postdoctoral-research-fellowships)
