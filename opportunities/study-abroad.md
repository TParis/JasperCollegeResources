# Study Abroad and Fulbright

> Use this page to compare language immersion, teaching experience, and scholarship paths without turning the decision into soup.

## Quick Answer

- For L&C programs, start with the official overseas program pages.
- Georgia and Kyrgyzstan are the most relevant Russian-language options here.
- Fulbright is usually later planning, but early experiences can strengthen an application.
- Verify current eligibility, dates, and prerequisites before relying on any program detail.

## L&C Overseas Programs

- [L&C Overseas and Off-Campus Programs](https://college.lclark.edu/programs/overseas_and_off-campus/programs/) — Main program directory.
- [Georgia: Tbilisi](https://college.lclark.edu/live/profiles/19956-georgia-tbilisi) — Russian language study, regional studies, and possible volunteer teaching.
- [Kyrgyzstan: Bishkek](https://college.lclark.edu/live/profiles/16486-kyrgyzstan-bishkek) — Russian-language immersion and interaction with local English learners.

## What to Do

- [ ] Ask an advisor how study abroad fits graduation requirements.
- [ ] Ask Dr. Histova which Russian courses to complete first.
- [ ] Check prerequisites and GPA requirements on the official page.
- [ ] Ask whether teaching, tutoring, or volunteering abroad can connect to credit or experience.
- [ ] Make a backup plan in case timing changes.

## Teaching Experience at L&C

- [Become a SAAB Tutor](https://college.lclark.edu/academics/support/saab-tutoring/becoming-a-tutor/) — Potential paid teaching experience after qualifying coursework.
- [Center for Social Change and Community Involvement](https://college.lclark.edu/student_life/social_change/) — Look for tutoring, youth education, and Portland-area service opportunities.

## Fulbright

- [Fulbright U.S. Student Awards](https://us.fulbrightonline.org/applicants/types-of-awards) — Main award category page.
- [Fulbright Open Study/Research Award](https://us.fulbrightonline.org/applicants/types-of-awards/study-research) — For a designed study or research project abroad.
- [Fulbright English Teaching Assistant Awards](https://us.fulbrightonline.org/applicants/types-of-awards/english-teaching-assistant-awards) — For teaching English abroad.
- [L&C Students and Alumni Receiving National Recognition](https://www.lclark.edu/live/news/11293-students-and-alumni-receive-national-recognition) — Examples of nationally competitive awards at L&C.

## Fulbright Planning Notes

- Begin exploring before senior year.
- Keep notes on teaching, tutoring, Russian study, study abroad, and community work.
- Compare Fulbright with [CLS](https://clscholarship.org/) if the goal is intensive summer Russian study.
