# Jobs and Internships

> This page is for finding work, building experience, and making future applications easier.

## Quick Answer

- Campus jobs are real experience.
- Tutoring and research can matter a lot for math and graduate school.
- The Career Center can help with resumes, internships, interviews, and planning.
- Keep a brag file now. Future you will be grateful.

## What to Do

- [ ] Check [Student Employment](https://www.lclark.edu/offices/human_resources/jobs/students/) for campus job rules and postings.
- [ ] Visit the [Career Center](https://college.lclark.edu/student_life/career_development/) early, not only senior year.
- [ ] Ask math faculty about research assistant or tutoring paths.
- [ ] Save every meaningful project, job, award, and presentation in a brag file.
- [ ] Update a simple resume once per semester.

## Resume-Fit Searches Near Campus

> These searches are aimed at jobs you could plausibly explain from your resume: tutoring, childcare, education support, project support, photography assistant work, and campus jobs. Use them as starting points, then check the commute before applying.

### Best First Searches

- [L&C Student Employment](https://www.lclark.edu/offices/human_resources/jobs/students/) — Campus jobs are the cleanest commute. Start here before wrestling the internet.
- [Handshake](https://app.joinhandshake.com/stu/postings) — Search for **tutor**, **student assistant**, **office assistant**, **research assistant**, and **education** roles near Portland.
- [Indeed: math tutor near 97219, 5-mile radius](https://www.indeed.com/jobs?q=math%20tutor&l=97219&radius=5) — Best match for math tutoring and homework-help roles.
- [Indeed: childcare near 97219, 5-mile radius](https://www.indeed.com/jobs?q=childcare%20babysitter%20after%20school&l=97219&radius=5) — Good match for babysitting, childcare, and after-school experience.
- [Indeed: student assistant near 97219, 5-mile radius](https://www.indeed.com/jobs?q=student%20assistant%20office%20assistant&l=97219&radius=5) — Good match for organized, part-time campus-adjacent work.

### Also Worth Checking

- [LinkedIn: tutor jobs near Portland](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/search/?keywords=tutor&location=Portland%2C%20Oregon%2C%20United%20States) — Useful for tutoring centers, education nonprofits, and formal part-time postings.
- [Care.com: tutoring jobs in Portland](https://www.care.com/tutoring-jobs/portland-or) — Good for private tutoring and family-based education help.
- [Care.com: babysitting jobs in Portland](https://www.care.com/babysitting-jobs/portland-or) — Good match for childcare experience, but screen carefully and meet safely.
- [Craigslist: education jobs in Portland](https://portland.craigslist.org/search/edu) — Sometimes useful for tutoring and education support. Be extra alert for scams.

### Commute Filter

Before applying, check:

- [ ] Is it on campus or close to campus?
- [ ] Is it roughly **10 minutes by Uber** or an easy bus ride?
- [ ] Does the shift end while transit is still running?
- [ ] Can you get back safely in the dark or rain?
- [ ] Does the job understand you are a student first?

## Keep a Brag File

Create one running note with:

- projects,
- research,
- tutoring,
- presentations,
- awards,
- leadership,
- work accomplishments,
- interesting coursework.

Do not wait until a resume deadline. Details disappear fast.

## Resume Basics

Keep it simple:

- education,
- work or volunteer experience,
- projects,
- skills,
- awards,
- activities.

Use action verbs and concrete results when possible.

## If Something Goes Wrong

If you have no experience yet, start small:

- one campus job,
- one club role,
- one tutoring or service activity,
- one project worth describing.

Repeated small things become a story.
