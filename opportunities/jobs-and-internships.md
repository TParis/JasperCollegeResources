# Jobs and Internships

> This page is for finding work, building experience, and making future applications easier.

## Quick Answer

- Campus jobs are real experience.
- Tutoring and research can matter a lot for math and graduate school.
- The Career Center can help with resumes, internships, interviews, and planning.
- Keep a brag file now. Future you will be grateful.

## What to Do

- [ ] Check [Student Employment](https://www.lclark.edu/offices/human_resources/jobs/students/) for campus job rules and postings.
- [ ] Visit the [Career Center](https://college.lclark.edu/student_life/career_development/) early, not only senior year.
- [ ] Ask math faculty about research assistant or tutoring paths.
- [ ] Save every meaningful project, job, award, and presentation in a brag file.
- [ ] Update a simple resume once per semester.

## Good Experience Buckets

| Goal | Useful Experience |
| --- | --- |
| Math PhD | Research, advanced coursework, talks, faculty relationships |
| Teaching | Tutoring, SAAB, classroom support, volunteer teaching |
| Language | Russian coursework, study abroad, conversation groups |
| Career | Campus jobs, internships, projects, LinkedIn, references |

## Keep a Brag File

Create one running note with:

- projects,
- research,
- tutoring,
- presentations,
- awards,
- leadership,
- work accomplishments,
- interesting coursework.

Do not wait until a resume deadline. Details disappear fast.

## Resume Basics

Keep it simple:

- education,
- work or volunteer experience,
- projects,
- skills,
- awards,
- activities.

Use action verbs and concrete results when possible.

## If Something Goes Wrong

If you have no experience yet, start small:

- one campus job,
- one club role,
- one tutoring or service activity,
- one project worth describing.

Repeated small things become a story.
