# Russian

> Russian opportunities work best when you plan coursework, practice, and applications together.

## Quick Answer

- Start with L&C Russian courses and regular practice.
- Ask Dr. Histova how to prepare for study abroad and CLS.
- Use Russian Club or conversation tables for repeated practice.
- Check official eligibility rules before assuming a program fits.

## Good First-Year Actions

- [ ] Ask which Russian sequence fits your current level.
- [ ] Attend Russian Club or conversation table more than once.
- [ ] Keep a list of useful vocabulary and grammar weak spots.
- [ ] Ask Dr. Histova what makes students ready for Georgia, Kyrgyzstan, or CLS.

## After Some Russian

Good next steps may include:

- language-intensive study abroad,
- the Critical Language Scholarship Program,
- Russian-related tutoring or cultural events,
- Fulbright planning later on.
