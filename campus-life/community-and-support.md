## Events and Activities

- [The Bark: Student News and Events](https://college.lclark.edu/student_life/current_students/)
  - The main source for current campus events, opportunities, deadlines, and announcements.

- [College Outdoors](https://www.lclark.edu/programs/college_outdoors/)
  - Weekend trips, hiking, rafting, kayaking, skiing, nature activities, and student-leadership opportunities.

- [OtterBus](https://www.lclark.edu/offices/transportation_and_parking/shuttle/)
  - Free campus shuttle with connections to Portland public transportation.

## Trivia and Karaoke

- [Multnomah French Quarter](https://www.multnomahfrenchquarter.com/)
  - Nearby food-cart venue that currently advertises trivia every Tuesday from 6:30-8:30 p.m.
  - Verify the schedule before traveling.

- [Voicebox Karaoke](https://voiceboxkaraoke.com/)
  - Private-room karaoke with Portland locations.
  - Guests under 21 are currently allowed before 9 p.m. Sunday-Thursday and before 7 p.m. Friday-Saturday.
