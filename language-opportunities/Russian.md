# Russian Language Opportunities

This page collects Russian-specific language study, scholarship, and study abroad links for Jasper.

## Lewis & Clark Study Abroad

- [Teaching and Study Abroad Resources](../teaching-study-abroad/resources.md)
  - Start here for Lewis & Clark overseas programs, including Georgia: Tbilisi and Kyrgyzstan: Bishkek.

- [Georgia: Tbilisi](https://college.lclark.edu/live/profiles/19956-georgia-tbilisi)
  - Russian language study, regional studies, travel within the Caucasus, and possible volunteer teaching opportunities.

- [Kyrgyzstan: Bishkek](https://college.lclark.edu/live/profiles/16486-kyrgyzstan-bishkek)
  - Russian-language immersion and interaction with local English learners and international Russian learners.

## Critical Language Scholarship

- [Critical Language Scholarship Program](https://clscholarship.org/)
  - A U.S. Department of State program for intensive summer study of critical languages.

- [CLS Russian](https://clscholarship.org/languages/russian)
  - Russian requires prior study, so Jasper should track L&C Russian coursework carefully before applying.
  - The program is especially relevant if Jasper wants a focused summer language immersion experience.

- [CLS Language Levels and Prerequisites](https://clscholarship.org/applicants/language-levels)
  - Use this to confirm the Russian level requirement before selecting an application level.

- [Dr. Maria Histova](../faculty/maria-histova.md)
  - Dr. Histova's faculty page notes her connection to the Critical Language Scholarship Program.

## Fulbright

- [Fulbright U.S. Student Awards](https://us.fulbrightonline.org/applicants/types-of-awards)
  - Main entry point for Fulbright U.S. Student Program award types.

- [Fulbright Open Study/Research Award](https://us.fulbrightonline.org/applicants/types-of-awards/study-research)
  - Relevant for students designing a study or research project abroad.

- [Fulbright English Teaching Assistant Awards](https://us.fulbrightonline.org/applicants/types-of-awards/english-teaching-assistant-awards)
  - Relevant for students interested in teaching English abroad after building language, teaching, and intercultural experience.
