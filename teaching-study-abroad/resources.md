# Teaching and Study Abroad Resources

## Lewis & Clark Programs

- [L&C Overseas and Off-Campus Programs](https://college.lclark.edu/programs/overseas_and_off-campus/programs/)

- [Georgia: Tbilisi](https://college.lclark.edu/live/profiles/19956-georgia-tbilisi)
  - Russian language study, regional studies, travel within the Caucasus, and possible volunteer teaching opportunities.
  - Prerequisites: minimum 2.5 GPA and completion of the Words and Numbers CORE requirement.

- [Kyrgyzstan: Bishkek](https://college.lclark.edu/live/profiles/16486-kyrgyzstan-bishkek)
  - Russian-language immersion and interaction between local English learners and international Russian learners.
  - Prerequisites: RUS 202, minimum 2.5 GPA, and completion of the Words and Numbers CORE requirement.

- [Russian Club and Conversation Table](https://college.lclark.edu/offices/interactive_learning_center/foreign-language-clubs-/russian-club-activities-/)
  - Useful preparation before applying for either program.
