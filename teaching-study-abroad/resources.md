## Fulbright

- [Fulbright U.S. Student Awards](https://us.fulbrightonline.org/applicants/types-of-awards)
  - Start here for the main award categories available through the Fulbright U.S. Student Program.
  - Most students compare the Study/Research Award and English Teaching Assistant Award first.
  - Jasper should connect with Lewis & Clark's Fulbright Program Adviser early, because enrolled students typically apply through their college or university.

- [Fulbright Open Study/Research Award](https://us.fulbrightonline.org/applicants/types-of-awards/study-research)
  - Best fit for students who want to design an independent research or study project abroad.
  - Applicants usually work with advisers at a foreign university, research institute, or other host organization.
  - Country requirements vary, so the country profile should be checked before planning a project.

- [Fulbright English Teaching Assistant Awards](https://us.fulbrightonline.org/applicants/types-of-awards/english-teaching-assistant-awards)
  - Best fit for students interested in teaching English abroad while building language, intercultural, and classroom experience.
  - This may connect well with Jasper's prior classroom experience and interest in language study.
  - Look carefully at country-specific placement expectations, language requirements, and teaching duties.

- [L&C Students and Alumni Receiving National Recognition](https://www.lclark.edu/live/news/11293-students-and-alumni-receive-national-recognition)
  - Useful for seeing how Lewis & Clark students and alumni have pursued nationally competitive awards.

### Fulbright Planning Notes

- Begin exploring Fulbright before senior year so there is time to build language preparation, faculty relationships, and a coherent project or teaching narrative.
- Keep notes on teaching, tutoring, Russian study, study abroad, and community work; these experiences can become useful application evidence.
- Compare Fulbright with the [Critical Language Scholarship Program](https://clscholarship.org/) if the immediate goal is intensive summer Russian language study.
- See [Russian Language Opportunities](../language-opportunities/Russian.md) for Russian-specific language links.
