# Exploring Portland

> Portland is easier to enjoy with a short list, a charged phone, and a return plan.

## Quick Answer

- Start with easy trips near shuttle or transit routes.
- Verify hours, age rules, and event schedules before leaving.
- Rain preparation is not optional.
- Keep the first trips simple.

## Before Going Somewhere New

- [ ] Check the route there.
- [ ] Check the route back.
- [ ] Check when service stops.
- [ ] Charge your phone.
- [ ] Bring rain gear if the forecast even hints at nonsense.

## Easy Categories

### Free or Cheap

- Parks and waterfront walks.
- Powell's Books.
- Campus events listed in The Bark.
- Student club events.

### Rainy-Day Ideas

- Bookstores.
- Coffee shops.
- Museums.
- Private-room karaoke with friends.

### Social Ideas

- Trivia nights.
- Live music.
- College Outdoors trips.
- Food carts or casual meals.

## Existing Notes

- [Multnomah French Quarter](https://www.multnomahfrenchquarter.com/) — Nearby food-cart venue that has advertised trivia. Verify current schedule before going.
- [Voicebox Karaoke](https://voiceboxkaraoke.com/) — Private-room karaoke with Portland locations. Verify current hours and under-21 rules before going.

## Easy Day Trips

Keep day trips simple until transit feels familiar:

- downtown Portland,
- Powell's and nearby food,
- museums,
- parks reachable by transit,
- campus-organized outdoor trips.
