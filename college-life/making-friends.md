# Making Friends

> Friendships usually come from repeated contact, not one magical conversation.

## Quick Answer

- Pick 1-3 recurring groups.
- Go repeatedly, even if the first meeting feels awkward.
- Use classes, clubs, meals, and study groups as repeated-contact spaces.
- Invite people to do specific, low-pressure things.

## What to Do

- [ ] Attend orientation events.
- [ ] Try Math Club, Russian Club, QSU, NDSU, or College Outdoors.
- [ ] Sit near people in class and say hello more than once.
- [ ] Ask one person to study after class.
- [ ] Say yes to reasonable invitations early in the semester.

## Low-Pressure Invitations

Specific is easier than vague:

- "Want to grab lunch after class?"
- "Want to work on this problem set at the SQRC?"
- "Want to go to Math Club together?"
- "Want to check out this campus event?"

## If It Feels Awkward

Awkward does not mean failure.

Most people are also trying to figure out where they fit. Repeated friendly contact works better than trying to be instantly impressive.
