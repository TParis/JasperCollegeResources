# Food and Adulting

> The point is not to become a perfect adult. The point is to keep life from turning into mystery laundry and expensive snacks.

## Quick Answer

- Buy food you will actually eat.
- Do laundry before the emergency outfit stage.
- Clean a little every week.
- Keep important documents replaceable.
- Do not let mail and packages become a side quest.

## Groceries

Before shopping:

- [ ] Check what food you already have.
- [ ] Plan 2-3 easy meals.
- [ ] Buy portable snacks.
- [ ] Avoid buying more than you can carry.
- [ ] Check expiration dates.

Good staples:

- pasta or rice,
- eggs or simple protein,
- frozen vegetables,
- yogurt or cheese,
- bread or tortillas,
- fruit that does not instantly betray you.

## Easy Meals

Use categories, not complicated recipes:

- microwave meals,
- sandwiches,
- pasta,
- rice bowls,
- breakfast foods,
- frozen vegetables plus protein,
- soup plus bread.

## Food Safety

When in doubt, do not gamble with suspicious leftovers.

Basic rules:

- refrigerate leftovers,
- wash hands,
- keep raw meat separate,
- do not eat food that smells wrong,
- clean spills before they become archaeology.

## Laundry

- [ ] Separate obvious problem items.
- [ ] Use a normal amount of detergent.
- [ ] Use cold water for most loads.
- [ ] Check pockets.
- [ ] Move laundry promptly.
- [ ] Do not leave clothes in machines indefinitely.

If an item looks delicate or expensive, check the tag.

## Cleaning

### Weekly

- [ ] Trash.
- [ ] Laundry.
- [ ] Bathroom basics.
- [ ] Bedding if needed.
- [ ] Clear food containers.
- [ ] Wipe obvious surfaces.

### Tiny Daily Reset

- [ ] Put dishes/trash where they belong.
- [ ] Clear one surface.
- [ ] Put tomorrow's essentials in one place.

## Mail and Packages

Use [L&C package guidance](https://college.lclark.edu/student_life/first-year-experience/getting-started/shipping-packages-to-campus/) for current move-in shipping rules.

For regular campus mail, check current Mail Services instructions before ordering anything important.

## Replacing Important Documents

Know how to replace:

- student ID,
- driver's license or state ID,
- insurance cards,
- bank cards,
- passport if applicable.

Keep photos or secure copies where appropriate.
