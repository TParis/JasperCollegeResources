# Oh Shit Guide

> For when something has gone wrong and Jasper needs the next step, not a lecture.

## Immediate Danger

**Call or text 911 immediately.**

On campus, call L&C Campus Safety at **503-768-7777**.

## Someone Is Unconscious or Not Breathing Normally

**Call 911 immediately.**

Do not wait to see if it gets better.

## Lost Wallet

- [ ] Freeze bank and credit cards.
- [ ] Check recent locations.
- [ ] Report lost campus ID.
- [ ] Replace driver's license or state ID if needed.
- [ ] Watch accounts for suspicious charges.

## Lost Phone

- [ ] Use Find My Device / Find My iPhone.
- [ ] Remotely lock it.
- [ ] Contact carrier if it may be stolen.
- [ ] Change important passwords if needed.
- [ ] Ask Campus Safety or venue lost-and-found.

## Locked Out

- [ ] Contact RA or residence hall staff.
- [ ] Contact Campus Living if needed.
- [ ] Use Campus Safety for urgent after-hours safety concerns.

## Missed the Last Bus

- [ ] Get somewhere safe and populated.
- [ ] Preserve phone battery.
- [ ] Check alternate TriMet routes.
- [ ] Consider rideshare, taxi, or trusted pickup.
- [ ] Contact someone if you feel unsafe.

## Stranded Downtown

- [ ] Move to a well-lit, populated place.
- [ ] Avoid draining phone battery.
- [ ] Arrange transportation before wandering.
- [ ] Tell a trusted person where you are.
- [ ] Call 911 if you are in danger.

## Stolen Property

- [ ] Report to Campus Safety if on campus.
- [ ] File a police report when appropriate.
- [ ] Gather serial numbers, photos, receipts, or tracking info.
- [ ] Check whether insurance applies.

## Harassment or Threats

If there is current danger, call 911 or Campus Safety.

If the danger is not immediate, use campus reporting/support:

- [Student Support Resources](https://college.lclark.edu/student_life/student_support_resources/)
- [Campus Safety](https://www.lclark.edu/about/campus_safety/)
- [Your Options: Sexual and Interpersonal Violence](https://www.lclark.edu/healthy-relationships/resources-for-students/survivors/your-options/)

## Intoxicated Friend

Do not leave them alone if they may be medically unsafe.

Call emergency services if they:

- are unconscious,
- cannot be awakened,
- are breathing slowly or irregularly,
- are vomiting repeatedly,
- look pale, blue, or clammy,
- may have taken other substances.

## Roommate Conflict

1. Talk directly when safe.
2. Establish clear expectations.
3. Involve RA or residential staff.
4. Escalate serious safety issues immediately.
