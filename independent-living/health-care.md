### Insurance

You will be using **TRICARE Select** through **TriWest Healthcare Alliance** in the TRICARE West Region.

Important basics:

- [TRICARE Select](https://www.tricare.mil/About/Changes/Plans/Select?p=1) is a self-managed preferred provider plan.
- [TriWest](https://tricare.triwest.com/) administers TRICARE in the West Region, which includes Oregon.
- TRICARE Select usually does **not** require referrals for most primary or specialty appointments.
- Some services may still require **pre-authorization**.
- To pay less, use TRICARE-authorized **network providers** when possible.
- Your **Uniformed Services ID card** is the proof of TRICARE coverage.

When in doubt, call TriWest before the appointment:

- **TriWest / TRICARE West:** [888-TRIWEST / 888-874-9378](tel:+18888749378)

## Insurance Basics

Keep a photo of your insurance card.

Useful terms:

- **In-network:** providers your plan usually covers better.
- **Out-of-network:** may cost more.
- **Copay:** fixed amount due for a visit or prescription.
- **Deductible:** amount you may pay before insurance covers more.
- **Referral:** approval or recommendation needed for some care.
- **Pharmacy:** where prescriptions are filled.

## Missing Class Because of Illness

Email professors early.

Say:

- that you are sick,
- what class or assignment is affected,
- what you are asking for,
- when you expect to follow up.

Check school policies when illness affects multiple classes.

## Mental Health

If it is urgent or after hours, use crisis support:

- Call Dad, mom, a friend, Aunt Lauren, Katie, anyone, really....you're worth it to all of us
- **988** for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline.
- Campus Safety at **503-768-7777** for on-campus immediate safety concerns.
- 911 for off-campus emergencies.

For non-emergency support, contact the Student Counseling Center.
