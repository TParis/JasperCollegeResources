# Health Care

> Health care is easier when Jasper knows where to go before feeling terrible.

## If You Are in Immediate Danger

**Call or text 911.**

If on campus, also contact [Campus Safety](https://www.lclark.edu/about/campus_safety/) at **503-768-7777** when appropriate.

## Where to Go

| Situation | Typical Option |
| --- | --- |
| Routine issue | Primary care / student health |
| Same-day non-emergency | Urgent care |
| Serious or life-threatening | ER / 911 |

## Lewis & Clark Resources

- [Student Health Center](https://www.lclark.edu/offices/health_center/) — On-campus physical and mental health care for registered students.
- [Student Counseling Center](https://www.lclark.edu/offices/counseling/) — Counseling, crisis counseling, groups, and referrals.
- [Student Support Resources](https://college.lclark.edu/student_life/student_support_resources/) — Where to start when a student needs help.
- [LGBTQIA+ Resources](https://college.lclark.edu/student_life/wellness/lgbtqia-resources/) — Queer and trans health and campus support links.

Verify current hours, fees, and appointment rules before relying on them.

## Insurance Basics

Keep a photo of your insurance card.

Useful terms:

- **In-network:** providers your plan usually covers better.
- **Out-of-network:** may cost more.
- **Copay:** fixed amount due for a visit or prescription.
- **Deductible:** amount you may pay before insurance covers more.
- **Referral:** approval or recommendation needed for some care.
- **Pharmacy:** where prescriptions are filled.

## Prescriptions

- [ ] Know medication names and doses.
- [ ] Refill before running out.
- [ ] Ask how to transfer a prescription to a Portland pharmacy.
- [ ] Keep prescriber contact information.
- [ ] Know what to do if a refill is delayed.

## Missing Class Because of Illness

Email professors early.

Say:

- that you are sick,
- what class or assignment is affected,
- what you are asking for,
- when you expect to follow up.

Check school policies when illness affects multiple classes.

## Mental Health

If it is urgent or after hours, use crisis support:

- **988** for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline.
- Campus Safety at **503-768-7777** for on-campus immediate safety concerns.
- 911 for off-campus emergencies.

For non-emergency support, contact the Student Counseling Center.
