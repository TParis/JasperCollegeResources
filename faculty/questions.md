# Questions for Faculty

> Bring a few good questions, not every question. Pick the ones that match the meeting.

## Quick Answer

- For advising, focus on course planning and graduation requirements.
- For Dr. Starvrov, focus on math preparation, research, and graduate school.
- For Dr. Histova, focus on Russian study, practice, study abroad, and scholarships.

## L&C Student Advisor

- Can you help me create a four-year academic plan that includes my Mathematics and Statistics coursework, Russian language study, and a semester abroad without delaying graduation?
- When should I begin planning and applying for the Georgia or Kyrgyzstan overseas programs, and what prerequisites should I complete first?
- Can teaching, tutoring, internships, or volunteer work completed during an overseas program count toward academic credit or graduation requirements?
- How does the student employment program work, and are there campus jobs related to mathematics, tutoring, education, research, or technology?
- Can my Texas teacher’s aide certification and previous classroom experience help me qualify for campus employment, tutoring, or education-related opportunities?
- What gender-affirming mental health and student-support services are available on campus?
- Can I use those health and counseling services while covered by TRICARE Select instead of the university health-insurance plan?
- Based on my goals, which professors, offices, or student organizations should I connect with during my first semester?

## Dr. Iva Starvrov

- If I am considering a PhD in mathematics, how early should I begin planning, and what should I prioritize during my first two years?
- Can a student apply directly to a mathematics PhD program after completing a bachelor’s degree, or is a master’s degree sometimes advisable first?
- Is the University of Oregon a strong option for a PhD in mathematics, and what other programs should I consider as I clarify my interests?
- Which undergraduate courses, research experiences, and extracurricular activities are most important for becoming a competitive PhD applicant?
- How does a mathematics student identify a research area? At what point should I begin narrowing my interests beyond simply enjoying mathematics?
- When should I begin looking for undergraduate research opportunities, summer research programs, or independent-study projects?
- Do you work with undergraduate students on research? If so, what preparation would I need before approaching you about a project?
- How does finding a doctoral advisor work, and what should a student look for when evaluating potential advisors and graduate programs?
- Are there local seminars, conferences, thesis defenses, professional organizations, or other events that undergraduate mathematics students are encouraged to attend?
- Are there opportunities for undergraduates to assist with teaching, tutoring, grading, or course preparation? I have a Texas teacher’s aide certification and previous classroom experience.

## Dr. Maria Histova

- What are the best self-study resources I can use alongside my Russian courses for vocabulary, grammar, listening, and conversation practice?
- Are there Russian-speaking communities, cultural organizations, conversation groups, or events in the Portland area that welcome students?
- Are there opportunities during either overseas program to tutor or teach English, volunteer in schools, or work with local students?
- Are there scholarships, language grants, summer programs, or other funding opportunities for students studying Russian abroad?
